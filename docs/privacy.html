Tumbli Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Short version: Tumbli does not collect, send or share any personal data. Everything it knows stays on your computer.

What Tumbli stores

To work, Tumbli saves a few things locally on your PC, inside its own app data folder:

  • your settings (daily goal, glass size, reminder interval, active hours, sound and startup choices),
  • how many glasses you logged each day, for the last 90 days, used for your streak and history chart,
  • where you placed the floating widget on your screen.

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling Tumbli removes it.

What Tumbli checks on your PC

To avoid interrupting you, Tumbli asks Windows how long ago you last used the keyboard or mouse (so it doesn't remind you while you're away) and whether a fullscreen app, game or presentation is in front (so it can hide). It only reads these yes/no and time values. It does not record keystrokes, screen contents or which apps you use.

Network, accounts and ads

Tumbli has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. It does not connect to any server run by us or by third parties.

Children

Tumbli does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
